Model Flow

Decision workflow
1Build route-month panel from public airport context and sourced route events.
2Score route opportunity using service viability, demand context, strategic fit, hub pressure, and evidence confidence.
3Simulate marketing response curves with adstock and saturation assumptions.
4Run sensitivity and recovery analysis to test channel ranking and budget direction.
5Optimize route-budget pairs under portfolio constraints.
6Validate recommendations with matched-route, geo-lift, or capacity-gated tests.

Claims And Guardrails

Interview-safe framing
The project supports route-level portfolio direction and experiment prioritization under public-data constraints. It does not claim observed causal channel ROI.
Future production pathBigQuery for route-month data, Vertex AI Pipelines for reproducible scoring, Meridian when real marketing spend and outcomes exist.
Primary missing dataRoute-level bookings, campaign spend, conversion events, load factor, and fare/yield guardrails.
Best interview pointBudget direction is more stable than exact channel ranking, so the recommendation must flow into experiments before scaling.
